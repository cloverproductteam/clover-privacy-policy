# Clover Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 19 October 2026

Clover helps you keep track of the little things that make each day special. Your privacy matters, and this policy explains how Clover handles your information.

## 1. Information Clover stores

Clover stores information you enter into the app locally on your device. This may include:

- Your written daily entries and their dates.
- Your lucky-day markers and entry timestamps.
- Your app settings, reminder preferences and onboarding status.
- Your Clover+ unlock status and review-prompt preferences.

Clover does not currently require an account. The app does not operate its own server to collect or store your journal entries, and it does not currently include its own analytics or advertising SDKs.

## 2. Notifications

If you allow notifications, Clover can schedule reminders locally on your device. You can change or disable reminders in the app's Settings or through your device's notification settings.

Clover does not currently send remote push notifications.

## 3. Face ID and Touch ID

If you enable biometric app protection, Clover uses your device's authentication system to request verification. Clover does not receive or store your Face ID or Touch ID biometric data.

## 4. Widgets

If you use Clover widgets, information displayed by a widget may be accessible on your device's Home Screen. Consider your device's privacy settings when choosing what information is visible.

## 5. Sharing entries

Clover lets you create and share a monthly recap image. You can choose which entries to include or hide entry text.

When you share an image, the image and any included text are passed to the app or service you select through your device's sharing features. That service's own privacy policy applies to its handling of the information.

## 6. Storage and backups

Clover stores app data locally using your device's app storage. Depending on your device settings and backup configuration, some app data may be included in an iCloud or computer backup.

Clover does not currently provide its own cloud-sync service. Clover cannot control how Apple or other backup providers handle data under their own services and settings.

Deleting Clover or changing your device's backups may affect your locally stored entries. Data retained in existing backups may not be deleted immediately.

## 7. Clover+ purchases

Clover plans to offer a one-time Clover+ purchase through Apple's in-app purchase system. When purchases are enabled, Apple processes transactions under its own terms and privacy practices. Clover does not receive your full payment card details.

Purchase handling and the information available to Clover may depend on the final implementation.

## 8. Data retention and deletion

Your entries remain in the app until you edit or delete them, clear the app's data, or remove the app, subject to device and backup behaviour.

Clover does not currently have user accounts or a separate server containing journal entries to delete. Backups may retain data according to your device and backup settings.

## 9. Children's privacy

Clover is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through a Clover-operated server. The app does not currently require an account.

Parents and guardians should consider the device's privacy settings and any information shared through other apps or services.

## 10. International users and legal rights

Clover is intended to be available internationally. Privacy rights and legal requirements vary by location. Depending on the laws that apply to you, you may have rights concerning access to, correction of, deletion of, or other handling of personal information.

Because Clover stores journal entries locally and does not currently operate an account-based data service, many requests can be handled directly within the app or through your device settings.

## 11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Clover's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at the privacy policy link provided in the app and on the app's store listing.
